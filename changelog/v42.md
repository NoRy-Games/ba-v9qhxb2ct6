# v42 — 2026-11-15
## Fights
For players who are not the host of the instance:
- Basic attacks and skills used in a quick chain no longer get lost. One that reached the host up to a quarter of a second before your previous action ended was thrown away; the host now keeps it and starts it at the right moment.
## Balance
- Each kill and each death now takes 30 seconds off the ultimate's wait, instead of 45.
## Lobby
- The SETTINGS wall moved to the wall under the stairs that go up.
## Performance
Nothing changes in how the game looks.
- Morioh: the city's buildings and vehicles use about 320 MB less video memory. The download is about 12 MB larger.
## Colosseum
- The scenery has sun shadows now, as in Morioh.
- The arena floor shows its paving at the right size instead of one stretched picture.
- Columns, blocks and other ruins are no longer dark: they have their light stone colour back. The Colosseum's brick has its original lighter tone.
- Amphorae are light grey instead of plain white, and a few blockers that should be invisible are no longer drawn.

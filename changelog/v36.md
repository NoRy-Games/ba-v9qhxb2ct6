- During a round it takes the player out of the match and back to the lobby; a bot takes their place.

## Unstuck
Runs on the player's own screen and never counts as a death.
- Fell out of the map (nothing underfoot, or below the arena): straight back to the last open floor they stood on.
- Trying to walk for 4 s without moving while off the open floor (inside a car, on a ledge with no way down): moved to the nearest open floor.
- Pushing a wall while standing on open floor does nothing.
- By hand: hold U (desktop) or keep both hands over your head (VR) for 10 s, standing still and not hit in the last 3 s.
  - Moves at least 1.5 m, to open floor.
  - Then 20 s before it can be used again.
- Never into the enemy team's base.
- Not while down, frozen, stunned, during the 3-2-1 or under the floor with Sticky Dive.
- HOW TO PLAY shows the control.

# v33 — 2026-11-09
## Bot AI (DIO, Jotaro, Jotaro (SO), Diavolo, Fugo)
Only the bots' behaviour changed; damage, timings and player rules stay the same.

| What the bot did | Now |
|---|---|
| Stopped time with the enemy ~10 m away | Only with the enemy close: Jotaro 5 m, Jotaro (SO) 5.6 m, DIO 7.5 m; never over an ally's time stop |
| Guarded during its own time stop | In its own time stop: attacks the closest, weakest frozen enemy |
| Guard and Vanishing of Time on every approach | Only when a nearby enemy is attacking and facing it |
| Diavolo used Vanishing of Time inside his own Crimson Dimension | Doesn't |
| DIO teleported whenever the target was over 5 m away | Only with the ultimate ready to follow, or to finish a target two punches from death; stops 1.5 m in front |
| Fugo fought inside his own virus | Killer Haze only with the target over 4.5 m away; fights at 2.8 m and leaves his own clouds |

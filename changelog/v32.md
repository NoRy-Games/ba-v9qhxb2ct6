# v32 — 2026-11-08
## Bots no longer walk "frozen" (visual only)
- After an attack or skill with no walking version, a bot that keeps walking moves its legs again after 0.3 s. Before, the body slid in the cast pose for 2 to 4 s.
- Sticky Dive exit and support skills: from ~1 s to ~0.3 s.
- Stunned or frozen by a time stop: the bot stops at once.

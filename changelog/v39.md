# v39 — 2026-11-13
## Smaller download
Nothing changes in how the game looks or plays.
- The world download is about 91 MB smaller: 687 MB instead of about 779 MB.
- Animations: bone positions that never move are stored once instead of on every frame.
- City blocks and characters: the data that shapes their lighting is stored more compactly.
- Textures that only belonged to interiors already removed from the world are no longer included.
- Textures that existed twice are now shared.
## Lobby
- The skill guide now says that Crazy Heal, Parts Maker and Cloud Barrier go to the nearest teammate in front of you in team modes.

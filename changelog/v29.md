# v29 — 2026-11-06
## Ultimate with a cooldown (every character and bot)
- Before: one ultimate per life, ready again on every respawn.
- Now: 2 minutes between uses, as many times as the round allows.
- Each kill takes 45 s off the wait. With under 45 s left, the ultimate is ready at once.
- The wait carries on after dying.
- Every round starts with all ultimates ready, after the 3 s spawn charge.
- Energy cost unchanged. Training still has no wait.
- A Stand theft that seals the victim's ultimate still lasts until they respawn (the HUD shows SEALED).

# v48 — 2026-11-18

Published to the private Morioh and Colosseum PC worlds.

## Fights

For players who are not the host:

- Firing again keeps earlier shots visible while their confirmations are still on the way.
- A follow-up attack no longer clears the prediction of a teleport that already happened locally, preventing its late confirmation from applying the move again.
- Arrival effects play when local movement happens. Stone Hook's arrival effect does not repeat when the host confirms it later.
- The next input can travel during the final part of its own confirmed action's wait, allowing for up to 250 ms of network travel. The host still enforces the full cooldown, energy cost and targeting checks.
- The local energy estimate accounts for the extra drain of a confirmed separated Stand, including Whitesnake's reduced drain.

Late cast confirmations for an empty combat slot are ignored.

## Connection diagnostics

- Rejected skills show a specific reason on the HUD, such as cooldown, energy, recovery, blocked origin, range or invalid placement.
- The local VRChat log records those reasons under `[AJB-CAST]`. The `[AJB-NETSTAT]` summary also records the time from local input to confirmation, counting local confirmations separately from other players' attacks.
- The extra diagnostics send no additional network messages.

## Validation

Local compiled tests passed with controlled 475 ms confirmations. Morioh passed all 24 isolated test groups, 75 match-flow checks and 181 skill checks. All 84 skill captures passed their file and provenance audit; a focused visual comparison of 14 affected profiles found no new regression against v47.

Colosseum passed 5 lobby checks and 75 match-flow checks. Its skill suite remains at 174/181, matching v47 with no newly failing checks. Two assertions expect Morioh draw distances rather than Colosseum's intentional 600 m setting. Five wall-climb and floor/ceiling Zipper checks still require targeted validation on Colosseum; they are not reported as passed or fixed by this release.

Review in a real multiplayer session and headset remains pending.

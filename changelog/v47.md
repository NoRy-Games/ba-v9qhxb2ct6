# v47 — 2026-11-17
## Effects
- Explosion sparks fly outward again on Road Roller, Sheer Heart Attack and Air Bomb, instead of standing still in the middle of the blast.
- Several effects that had become fully opaque are see-through again, as in the original: the Sex Pistols wind spiral (Pistols Strike and Rodeo Shot), Needle Shot's impact flash and glow, and the stun flash.
- Joseph's Overdrive fire aura has its original strength back.
- Distortion effects such as Emerald Splash's charge now have their original size, duration and movement.
- Bubble Cutter's charge shows the original number of bubbles.
## Performance
- The game scripts do much less work every frame. In our tests a round with 8 fighters spends about 40 % less processor time on them, and the lobby is lighter too. Everything looks and plays the same.

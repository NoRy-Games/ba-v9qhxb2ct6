# v43 — 2026-11-16
## Performance
Nothing changes in how the game looks once the textures around you have loaded.
- Textures now load at the size your view needs instead of all at full size, in both worlds. In our tests at 1080p, Morioh's textures went from about 1.3 GB of video memory to about 450 MB in the lobby and about 550 MB in a full fight.
- When you enter the world or teleport far, the textures around you sharpen within about 3 seconds.
- When the graphics card is short on memory, textures stay at a lower resolution (a quarter at the lowest) instead of filling it.
- The fighters and Stands of the round ask for their textures at full resolution.
- The download size does not change.
## Lobby
- The SETTINGS wall shows how many textures are loaded at full, half and quarter resolution.

# v40 — 2026-11-13
## Controls
- A skill or basic attack pressed up to 0.3 seconds too early now fires as soon as it can, instead of being lost.
- VR controllers vibrate when you cast, when you land a hit and when you take damage.
## Fights
- For visitors, bots are drawn closer to where they really are: about 0.4 seconds behind instead of about 0.6.
- A round no longer freezes for a moment when it starts: fighters are set up one after another instead of all at once.
## Lobby
- The stairs are open: the whole building can be walked, with solid railings and no way to fall or to leave.
- A mirror covers one wall of the side corridor. Its buttons are at the left end: OFF, LOW, MEDIUM and HIGH. Only you see it, and it turns itself off when you leave that room or enter a fight.
- The ARENA LIVE wall does no work for players who are fighting.
## Settings
- New DISTORTION row under GRAPHICS: ON, LOW or OFF for the effects that bend the picture, such as heat haze. Only for you.
- In VR these effects bent the picture almost twice as much as on a desktop screen. They now look the same in both.
- PERFORMANCE turns distortion off by itself; press ON or LOW to keep it.
- PERFORMANCE also stops drawing the houses and furniture of the combat area beyond 100 m. They fade into the haze first; the ground, fighters and effects are always drawn.
- Inside the lobby, BALANCED and PERFORMANCE draw the city only up to 50 m.
## Performance
Nothing changes in how the game looks.
- Characters and Stands cost less every frame: their bone lengths are kept by the animation itself instead of being rewritten each frame.
## Plaza
- A turtle named Nova rests on a rock in the fountain.
## World photo
- The world has a new picture: Jotaro and DIO facing each other in the plaza.

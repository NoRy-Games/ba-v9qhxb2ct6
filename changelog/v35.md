# v35
## Natural bot aim
- Each bot shot aims at a point that follows the target about 0.14 s behind, with about 2° of error either way.
- Hits almost always at 6 m, about 3 in 4 at 12 m and 1 in 3 at 20 m.
- EASY and NORMAL still miss more than HARD.
- Melee is unchanged.
## Diavolo / King Crimson — Crimson Dimension
| | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Erased time | 3.5 s | **6.5 s** |

For the whole erased time:
- Diavolo can't be hit;
- everyone within 50 m moves at half speed;
- Diavolo's Rush is locked.
## Bruno Bucciarati / Sticky Fingers — Sticky Dive
- The player dives 2.2 m under the floor (deeper for tall avatars) and is hidden by it.
- Underground they see the arena all around them, as if standing on the floor, with a slight blue tint.
- Follows the floor above (steps up to 0.8 m, slopes up to 70°) and can't pass under walls.
- Can't attack or use the special while under the floor. Bots too.
- After 6 s they come back up onto the floor.
- Unchanged: immune, 1.8× faster, and nobody can aim at them.
## Risotto Nero / Metallica — Ironsand Camouflage
- The avatar no longer shrinks.
- On each enemy's screen, a cylinder around him shows the scenery behind him, with a slight ripple.
- Allies, anyone detecting him with CO2 Scan, and anyone right next to him still see him.
- Still lasts up to 20 s, spending 40 energy per second.
## Balance file
- These values now come from this repository's `balance.json`, with no new build needed:
  - damage and durations;
  - wait between uses, energy, range and radius;
  - barrage shot counts;
  - ultimate rules.
- Without a valid file the queue stays closed and the lobby shows why.
- On the BALANCE FILE strip under the HOST wall, the host or co-host can:
  - use the build's values (BUILT-IN);
  - reload the file (RELOAD).

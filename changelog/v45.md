# v45 — 2026-11-16
## Performance
- Small scenery far away is no longer drawn once it would cover only a few pixels on screen, in both worlds. In our tests the streets of Morioh draw about 13 % fewer objects on a desktop screen and about 20 % fewer with a wide view like a headset's, and the picture looks the same.
- The cut follows your VRChat graphics quality: on lower quality, small objects stop being drawn a little earlier.

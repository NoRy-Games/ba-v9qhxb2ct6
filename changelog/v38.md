# v38 — 2026-11-12
## Ultimate cooldown
- Dying no longer makes the ultimate ready: the 2 minute wait carries over into your next life.
- Each kill and each death takes 45 seconds off the wait.
## Performance
Nothing changes in how the game plays.
- Busy rounds run lighter: with 8 fighters, the world's scripts take about 13% less time per frame.
  - Skill effects: about half the work they did before.
  - Combat sounds and held weapons: less work every frame.
- The lobby does less work while nobody is fighting.
- The ARENA LIVE wall stops drawing its camera while it is behind you.
## City in the combat area
- On BALANCED and PERFORMANCE graphics, houses inside the combat area no longer vanish while their furniture stays floating in the air.
- A few books and a cup that floated inside houses are gone.
- 64 street pieces that were flat white now have the game's concrete texture.
- Window frames now have the right shine.
- The ground stays sharp across the whole combat area; it no longer turns blurry in the outer streets.
- Walls, floors and roofs use the original colours and texture scale: inside walls get their light paint with a dark skirting, wood bands and ground grime come back, and tiled walls show their relief.
- Tiled and wooden floors inside houses run along the walls instead of diagonally.
- Shop shelves, furniture and products have the original shine, and the original colour adjustments are applied in the original order.
## Characters
- Stands, and the bots' skin and clothes, use the original base colours; Stands have their glowing outline.
- Stands and bots now have the original surface detail: relief, shading in folds and creases, and shine on metal parts.
- Eyes use the original, slightly darker colours.
- Purple Haze's helmet is see-through again.
## Animations
- 13 skills play only the part of the animation the original plays, at its speed; for example, Instant Wall and First Bomb end sooner.
- Echoes ACT1 walks, and Hol Horse's Snipe plays at full speed.
- Road Roller: the ride and the final blow follow the original timing.
- Crazy Heal: Crazy Diamond plays its healing animation while it charges.
- Killer Haze, Red Bind and Air Bomb's Stand animations play at the original speed.
- Road Roller: DIO jumps before the roller comes down, as in the original.
- Glimpse of Force: The World finishes its animation when the teleport ends, instead of playing it to the end.
Bots (players keep their own avatar):
- Caesar's Bubble Launcher plays its own animation (it played Bubble Cutter's).
- Joseph, Mista and Hol Horse's basic attacks no longer mix in other skills' animations.
- Kakyoin's body now moves during Emerald Splash.
- Running diagonally forward and to the right uses the right animation for 26 characters.
- Stunned characters play the stunned animation.
- Seven characters walk and run with their original style, and several get their own defeat, crouch, landing and dash poses.
- Ruthless Bloodsuck plays the original animation.
- Vampire Healing plays DIO's healing animation, and Hol Horse's Emperor Typhoon opens at the original speed.
## Skill effects
Effect colours, fades and motion now follow the original particle data (about 590 emitters in 160 effects):
- Explosions (Sheer Heart Attack, Air Bomb, First Bomb, Cross Fire Hurricane and others) have their original red-orange colour instead of white-yellow.
- The common hit effect throws its sparks outward, with its violet core and flash.
- Fades in and out, random speeds and sizes, drag and trails behind moving effects match the original more closely.
- Effects that were cut short by a particle limit, and short effects that repeat in the original (Pistols Strike, Rodeo Shot and others), now play in full.
- Sparks and blood fall, and dust and smoke rise, with the original pull; explosion sparks fly outward again.
- Smoke, debris and sparks spread over the original area instead of starting from one point: Road Roller's explosion, Instant Wall breaking, the end of Rage and other buffs.
- Instant Wall debris and explosion sparks bounce off the ground and walls.
- Fades and colour changes keep their original shape more closely, and several dense effects (Emerald Splash's area, Heaven's Lock, Pink Dark's trap, defeat) emit more particles, closer to the original.
- Buff effects shown on the body (Rage, Bring It On, OverDrive and others) use the same fixed colours and motion.
- Over Grow's growing effect keeps going for its whole duration.
- Emerald Splash: the glow at Hierophant Green's hands keeps going while you fire, instead of restarting with every shot.
- Projectile trails (Emerald Splash, Sex Pistols, Emperor, Killer Haze, Bubble Launcher and others) are streaky trails that thin out toward the tail.
- Many hit flashes, shields, rings and smoke puffs draw with their original shape instead of a plain glowing square.
- Heat-haze distortion is back: about 60 effects bend the view behind them as in the original, among them the common hit, Dubbing, Vanishing of Time, Ironsand Camouflage, Air Bomb, Road Roller, Cross Fire Hurricane, Metallica and Snake Shot.
- Gold Experience Requiem's ring bends the view behind it instead of showing a white disc.
- Effects whose original colour is brighter than white keep both their colour and their glow: Volare Bomb is red-orange and Assault Dive violet, instead of washing out to white.
- Assault Dive's speed lines no longer show as a hard violet rectangle: they dissolve as in the original.
- Particles that circle around in the original now do: Emperor Typhoon's end clouds turn around Hol Horse, and the sparks of Rage, Bring It On, OverDrive and other buffs sway as they rise.
- Turbulence from the original stirs the sparks and dust of Requiem, Ironsand Camouflage, Needle Shot, the defeat effect and others.
- Crimson Dimension's trail behind slowed players is one smooth ribbon, bright at the front and thinning toward the tail, and it no longer stops halfway. OverDrive's faint trail at Joseph's hands is a ribbon too.
- Needle Shot no longer shows a large blue ball at the muzzle.
- About 90 more glows, smoke puffs and sparks reach their full original strength instead of looking washed out: Requiem's rings, Over Grow, Space Shaving, Gang Readiness, and the smoke of Bites the Dust, Rage and OverDrive among them.
- Lightning (Hamon hair, OverDrive, Bubble Lens, Sex Pistols' curves) is bright red or yellow, with no dark streaks.
- Standing inside the blast of Air Bomb, Volare Bomb, Killer Queen's First Bomb or Road Roller's explosion now makes the view ripple around you; from far away the bubble no longer shows.
- Over Grow: Gold Experience stays crouched with its fist on the ground while the tree grows, then stands up.
- About 80 more effects dissolve the way the original does instead of showing solid shapes, among them Diver Shoot's shock wave, Over Grow's opening, Pistols Strike's hit, Requiem's end flame, Rodeo Shot's impact smoke, the clouds when Sheer Heart Attack, Volare Bomb and Cross Fire Hurricane disappear, the buff auras on the body, and several shock waves and muzzle flashes.
- The fire and smoke where a character is defeated, and the flames when Rage, Gang Readiness, OverDrive and Bring It On start, take the shape of their flames and fade away, instead of a straight pink band and a black column.
New effects from the original:
- Time stop: the shrinking circle when it starts, and faint frozen streaks of wind around you while time is stopped.
- Defeat: a flash and smoke where the character falls, and the Stand fading away.
- Damage over time: the virus splash on each tick, emerald wires inside Emerald Splash's area, fire on burning characters, Hamon sparks.
- Snake Shot's ring when the bullet turns, Sheer Heart Attack fading when it ends without exploding, and Pink Dark's trap going off.
- Homing shots (Rodeo Shot, Pistols Strike, Snake Shot, Air Bomb) now curve toward their target for every player, not only for the instance owner.
- These effects show on everyone, players included, except on yourself.
- Joseph's OverDrive now shows on players too (it showed only on bots), except to the player using it.
- On other players, Rage, Bring It On, Gang Readiness and OverDrive cover their avatar's body (head, arms, hands, legs) as they do on bots.
- Rage, Bring It On, Gang Readiness and OverDrive cover the bots' bodies as in the original, on the same body parts (head, hands, thighs).
- Emperor's effect on Hol Horse bots stays on his arms, hands and legs; parts of it floated up to a metre away.
- Most High, Ironsand Camouflage, Vanishing of Time, the Crimson Dimension slow trail and the defeat effect now sit on the body's limbs as in the original (bots, and other players' avatars).
- Character detail textures are packed more tightly, for a smaller download with no visible change.
## Team modes
- Crazy Heal, Parts Maker and Cloud Barrier now go to the nearest teammate within 3 m in front of you, like in the original. With no teammate close, they still work on you. Free-for-all and 1V1 are unchanged.
- When you drop to 0 health in a team match while a teammate is alive, you are downed for 10 seconds instead of dying. A teammate who stands next to you for 4 seconds revives you with 30% health. If nobody comes, you are defeated as usual. Bots come to help downed teammates too.
## Network
- Hits between players register sooner: an attack from a player who is not the instance owner now lands on the owner's side closer to the moment it was made, hits on the instance owner match what attackers see, and hit confirmations are sent more often.
- Hurt and death voices, damage-over-time effects and the downed marker on other players now arrive together with their health bar instead of a moment later.
